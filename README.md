# Project Proposal: CampusCollab

## Team Name
**Team CampusCollab**
- Yusuf (Ostim Technical University - MIS 457)

## 1. The Problem
University students frequently need teammates with complementary skills for semester projects, hackathons, and study groups. Currently, students rely on crowded WhatsApp groups where announcements quickly get lost, making it difficult to find reliable partners with the right technical or business background.

## 2. Target Users
- **Undergraduate students** looking for study buddies or course project teammates.
- **Hackathon & competition leaders** recruiting developers, designers, or business analysts.
- **Student club mentors** organizing peer-learning study sessions on campus.

## 3. Core Features
1. **Project & Study Board (Browse & Filter):** Students can browse active team listings in a responsive card grid and filter them by category or required skill.
2. **Create a Team Listing:** Team leaders can publish a new listing specifying the project title, required skills, target deadline, and maximum number of available seats.
3. **Seat Reservation & Application Form:** Students can apply to join a team with a validated form; the system tracks remaining open seats automatically.
4. **User Authentication & Management:** Verified students can log in, edit their own listings, and manage incoming teammate requests.
